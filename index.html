Streakline

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026

Streakline is a Tic-Tac-Toe game with local game progress, customizable settings, and advertising. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

Streakline stores the following information locally on your device so game features work:

  • the player name and arena name you choose;
  • game statistics, including wins, losses, draws, XP, levels, and streaks;
  • theme, sound, and haptic-feedback preferences; and
  • the date and time used to calculate daily streaks.

Streakline does not require an account and does not use the locally stored game information to identify you. The app does not send this local game information to the developer.

Advertising

Streakline uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. When ads are loaded or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device or online identifiers, IP-address-derived approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostic information. This processing is used to provide ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where permitted, personalize advertising.

Advertising partners handle information under their own privacy policies and may use it across apps and websites as permitted by law and your device or account settings. You can manage applicable advertising controls in your device and Google account settings.

Internet access

The game itself can be played offline. Internet access is used for advertising services. If an advertisement cannot be loaded, core game features remain available.

How information is shared

The developer does not sell, rent, or share your locally stored player name, arena name, game statistics, preferences, or streak data. Information handled by Google Mobile Ads is shared with Google and its advertising partners only as described in the Advertising section above.

Data retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall Streakline. Android device backup settings may separately back up app data to your selected backup service. To remove local information, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings. The app also lets you reset score records in its settings.

Requests concerning information processed by advertising partners should be directed to the relevant partner under its privacy policy.

Children

Streakline does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Because the app displays third-party advertising, the developer will configure advertising and obtain any consent required by applicable law for the app's selected audience and regions.

Security

Local game data is stored in the app's private storage area provided by Android. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The current version will be posted at the privacy-policy link listed on the app's Google Play listing.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer at info@panabotics.com.
