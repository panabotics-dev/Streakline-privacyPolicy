Streakline: Tic Tac Toe & XO

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17-11-2026

Streakline: Tic Tac Toe & XO ("Streakline" or "the app") is a Tic Tac Toe game with local game progress, customizable settings and advertising. It is provided by Panabotics ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

Streakline stores the following information locally on your device so that game features work:

  • the player names you choose;
  • game statistics, including wins, losses, draws, XP, levels and streaks;
  • theme, sound and haptic-feedback preferences; and
  • the date and time used to calculate daily streaks.

Streakline does not require an account and does not use this locally stored game information to identify you. The app does not send this local game information to Panabotics.

Advertising

Streakline uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements. When ads are loaded or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device or online identifiers, IP-address-derived approximate location, app interactions and diagnostic information. This processing is used to provide ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse and, where permitted, personalize advertising.

Where applicable law requires your consent for advertising, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, consent choices are requested before ads that rely on such information are shown.

Advertising partners handle information under their own privacy policies and may use it across apps and websites as permitted by law and your device or account settings. You can manage advertising controls in your device settings and your Google account settings, including resetting your advertising ID or opting out of ad personalization.

Internet access

The game itself can be played offline. Internet access is used for advertising services. If an advertisement cannot be loaded, core game features remain available.

How information is shared

We do not sell, rent or share your locally stored player names, game statistics, preferences or streak data. Information handled by Google Mobile Ads is shared with Google and its advertising partners only as described in the Advertising section above.

Data retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall Streakline. Android device backup settings may separately back up app data to the backup service you selected. To remove local information, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings. The app also lets you reset score records in its settings.

Requests about information processed by advertising partners should be directed to the relevant partner under its privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal information held about you, such as access, correction or deletion. Because Streakline keeps your game data only on your own device and does not collect it, you can manage it yourself as described above. For information handled by advertising partners, you can use the controls in your device and Google account settings. If you have a privacy question or request, contact us using the details below.

Children

Streakline is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before using the app. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help resolve it.

Security

Local game data is stored in the app's private storage area provided by Android. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. When we do, we will update the effective date above. The current version is available at the privacy policy link on the app's Google Play listing.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Panabotics at info@panabotics.com.
